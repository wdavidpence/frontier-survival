Frontier Survival · living-room mode

Play on the big screen

Choose Solo for a laptop browser, or Local Co-op for two PS5 controllers on an iPhone mirrored to your TV.

1 · Cast your iPhone

Open Safari, load the game, then open Control Center → Screen Mirroring → choose your compatible AirPlay TV or Apple TV. Keep the iPhone awake and use landscape.

2 · Pair DualSense

On each PS5 controller, hold the PS button + Create button until the light bar flashes. On iPhone: Settings → Bluetooth → tap “DualSense Wireless Controller”. Repeat for controller two.

3 · Choose Local Co-op

Select Local Co-op. Player 1 uses controller 1 and Player 2 uses controller 2. Split screen is controller-only; keyboard and mouse are reserved for Solo laptop play.

4 · Reach other islands

Open Pack and Craft, make a Boat from wood, equip it, and swim into the ocean. Boats move faster in water; explore tropical islands, reefs, kelp, and beaches.
