## Arrival

Fresh worlds start on the Las Croabas sand shelf and look along the beach toward open water, with Fajardo municipio inland and the Spanish Virgins as voyage targets.

## Scale

- **Las Croabas beach and Fajardo municipio land: 10 m per cell (1:10).** Real municipal land is ~8.7 km E–W by 13.3 km N–S (~870 × 1330 cells). The streamed world only generates nearby chunks, so this is walkable.
- East water (Vieques Passage) stays open so Icacos and Palomino remain short voyage cays. Culebra, Vieques, and the rest of Puerto Rico stay compressed.
- Vertical: compressed into the 48-block world. Peaks are silhouettes, not 500 m Sierra de Luquillo ridges.

## Fajardo municipio mapping

Walk west/south from Cabezas onto `fajardo-municipio`, `fajardo-ridge`, `fajardo-interior`, `fajardo-west`, and `fajardo-south`. Place cues: Las Croabas, Seven Seas, Faro Cabezas, Fajardo Pueblo, Sardinera, Demajagua, Río Arriba, Río Fajardo, Laguna Grande.

## Rivers and caves

- **Río Fajardo** is a 10 m/cell water corridor from western foothills to the east coast south of town, with a **Río Demajagua** fork.
- **Laguna Grande** is an inland lagoon west of Cabezas (playable analogue, not GIS-true on the north cape).
- Underground caves already carve under land taller than sea+2. The new interior hills are thick enough for worm caves and rooms. The authored Las Croabas sea cave is unchanged.

Named bays, boat channels, and the Las Croabas water bowl stay open. Isolated 1-block land/water holes still fill.
