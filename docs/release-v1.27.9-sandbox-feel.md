# Frontier Survival v1.27.9 — Sandbox Feel

Minecraft-class interaction juice on the existing tropical cove:

- mined, Q-dropped, and death items bounce, spin, magnet, and collect
- sneak lowers the camera, shortens the body, and clings to ledges
- eating takes a chew window with crumbs
- unsupported leaves decay after their log is gone
- footstep dust, water-exit splash, underwater bubbles, mine-punch SFX, hurt knockback, hotbar name fade, and pickup pops

This is a sandbox-feel checkpoint, not Minecraft-class atmosphere parity.
